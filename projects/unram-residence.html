Project Overview

Not every construction project begins with an empty site or a new architectural design.

For UNRAM Residence, our client approached Atelier Nusa with an existing architectural design and construction budget. However, the initial Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and cost estimates required a comprehensive review to establish a more reliable basis for construction.

Our team reassessed the quantities, recalculated the construction costs, and reorganized the budget to support the project's execution.

The Challenge

One of the key challenges was the existing condition of the site.

Rather than starting construction on vacant land, the project required the complete demolition of an existing residence before the new structure could be developed.

The proposed building consists of two floors, with construction planned in separate phases.

This approach allows the project to progress gradually, beginning with the ground floor before continuing with the upper floor in a subsequent stage.

Construction & Project Delivery

Following the planning and cost assessment process, our team proceeded with the demolition and construction of the first phase.

The ground floor incorporates a contemporary architectural character, combining clean white surfaces, dark accents, glass openings, and carefully executed material details.

Throughout the construction process, our focus remained on maintaining workmanship quality and delivering a result aligned with the agreed design.

Phase 01 — Completed

The first phase of UNRAM Residence has now been successfully completed and handed over to the client.

We are grateful for the trust placed in Atelier Nusa and the positive feedback received on the completed work.

Every project brings valuable experience and motivates our team to continuously improve our planning, coordination, and construction quality.

We look forward to the next stage of this residence's development.
