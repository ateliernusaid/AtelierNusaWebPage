Atelier Nusa is an architecture and construction studio in Lombok, shaping homes, villas, interiors, renovations, and hospitality projects from the first concept through to the finished building. Our studio is based in Mataram and works across Lombok and NTB.

01

Architecture for homes, villas, and hospitality

We begin by understanding the land and the brief: how the building will be approached, where it should open up, what needs privacy, and what the budget can carry. From the first layout to detailed construction drawings, we develop a design that is clear enough to build and specific enough to feel like yours.

02

Construction management

We coordinate the practical side of the build: materials, trades, site decisions, and quality checks. When a condition changes on site, the design context is already familiar to the team, so the response can be discussed with the right people instead of passed between offices.

Why It Works

What it means to keep the work together

A shared studio does not remove the difficult parts of a project. It does make it easier to see them early and deal with them directly.

Site-First Thinking

Every design starts with the land. We study sun angles, prevailing winds, views worth framing, and privacy needs before putting anything on paper.

Fewer gaps between drawing and site

The people who worked through the design stay connected to the build. That gives site questions a shorter route back to the decisions behind the drawings.

Materials that make sense here

Heat, humidity, salt air, rain, cleaning, and replacement all matter. We consider those everyday conditions before choosing materials and details for a project.

Step by Step

A project, step by step

1

First conversation and site visit

We talk through the land, your needs, the likely budget, and what is still unclear. If the project is ready for a site visit, we look at the conditions that will affect the design and the build.

2

Concept design

We test the basic arrangement of the building: where it sits, how people move through it, which rooms get the best light, and what the first material direction might be. You can react to the important choices before detail takes over.

3

Detailed design and drawings

Once the direction is right, we resolve the details: dimensions, structure, services, materials, and the information the construction team needs to price and build the work.

4

Construction and handover

During construction, we coordinate progress and deal with questions as they arise. At the end, we walk through the completed work together, note anything that needs attention, and hand over a building ready for its next chapter.
