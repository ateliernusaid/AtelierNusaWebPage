Why Lombok

Designing for Lombok's climate and culture

Live with the tropical climate

Lombok's sun and humidity are beautiful but demanding. We design deep overhangs, natural cross-ventilation and shaded outdoor rooms, so your house feels cool without relying entirely on air conditioning.

Site-responsive architecture

Every plot is different — a cliff above the sea, a rice-field edge or a compact urban land. We read the site first and design with it, protecting views, gardens and privacy from the very first sketch.

Materials that age well

We balance local stone, timber, concrete and lightweight structures that tolerate the salt air and rain. The result is a low-maintenance building that grows more beautiful as it blends into the landscape.
