# Atelier Nusa Organic Growth Playbook

This is the operating plan for growing organic discovery, qualified traffic, and consultation leads without relying on paid ads.

## Existing Search Map

The current route set already covers the main commercial intents:

- `jasa arsitek lombok`
- `arsitek mataram`
- `arsitek kuta mandalika`
- `arsitek villa senggigi`
- `desain interior lombok`
- `biaya jasa arsitek lombok`
- `biaya bangun rumah lombok`

Do not create more location pages until each existing page has original project evidence, a local point of view, and a clear internal link to a related project or consultation page.

## First 30 Days

### Business profile

- Verify and complete Google Business Profile.
- Use the real studio address, service areas, phone, hours, website, and WhatsApp.
- Add the most accurate primary category and relevant secondary categories.
- Upload 10-15 original images: studio, survey, drawings, construction progress, materials, and completed work.
- Publish one GBP update each week.
- Request genuine reviews after a completed milestone. Never offer incentives or write reviews for clients.

### Measurement

- Verify `ateliernusa.id` in Google Search Console.
- Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Confirm GA4 events for `whatsapp_click` and `lead_submit`.
- Use these campaign labels:
  - `utm_source=threads&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=organic-distribution`
  - `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=organic-distribution`
  - `utm_source=google-business&utm_medium=local&utm_campaign=organic-discovery`
- Review impressions, non-brand clicks, CTR, landing pages, and qualified leads every month.

### Proof assets

Publish three case studies using this structure:

1. Site and project type.
2. The client's brief and constraints.
3. Climate/site observations.
4. Key design decisions.
5. Construction or delivery status.
6. Original photos and drawings.
7. A consultation CTA.

## Social Distribution

Use the same project evidence across Threads, Instagram, Pinterest, and short video. One post should contain:

- One specific observation or lesson.
- One original visual.
- One useful link, tagged with UTM parameters.
- One question that invites a real response.

Suggested weekly rhythm:

- Monday: site or climate lesson.
- Wednesday: project detail or construction progress.
- Friday: cost/process FAQ.
- Weekend: completed work, studio life, or client-approved testimonial.

## Link and Partnership Targets

Prioritize relevant local relationships over bulk directories:

- Lombok property and villa operators.
- Land agents and hospitality consultants.
- Material suppliers and craftsmen.
- Local architecture or construction communities.
- NTB media and tourism publications.
- Universities, workshops, and design events.

Ask partners for useful editorial mentions, project collaborations, or case-study references. Avoid paid link networks and mass directory submissions.

## Editorial Rules

- Never publish a fabricated project, client, review, statistic, or budget.
- Label images as concept, ongoing, or completed.
- Keep Indonesian acquisition pages distinct from the English brand pages.
- Prefer one strong case study over several thin location pages.
- Keep the studio address, phone, email, and brand name identical everywhere.
