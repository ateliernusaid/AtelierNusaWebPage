Design & Build

Contractor & Construction Mataram

Building is a sequence of ordinary decisions made carefully: what to order, when to order it, who needs to be on site, and what needs checking before the next trade begins. We coordinate those decisions across the project and keep the plan grounded in local conditions.

How We Work

Start with the place

Lombok gives every project a different set of conditions. A coastal site needs a different response to a shaded hillside or a busy residential street. We look at sun, rain, airflow, views, and maintenance early, then use those findings to decide where rooms, openings, shade, and outdoor space should go.

The studio

A practical way of working

The site comes first

A beachfront villa in Kuta Mandalika has different demands from a family home in Mataram or a hillside retreat in Senggigi. We use the site to make those differences useful, instead of forcing every project into the same plan.

Design stays connected to the build

The team working through the design remains close to the construction. It makes it easier to clarify a detail, adjust a decision, and keep the finished building close to the intention behind the drawings.

We work from Lombok

We are based in Mataram and work across the island, from Senggigi and the Gili Islands to Kuta Mandalika and East Lombok. Being close to the work helps us make decisions with the actual site, supply chain, and weather in view.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does architect services cost in Lombok?

Architect services at Atelier Nusa start from Rp 150,000 - Rp 350,000 per square meter, depending on design complexity and project scale. The initial consultation is completely free and without commitment.

How long is the design and construction process?

The design process usually takes 4-8 weeks, while construction ranges from 6-18 months depending on the project scale. We provide a detailed timeline at the beginning of the project so you can plan everything.

Do you serve projects outside Mataram?

Yes, we serve all areas in Lombok including Senggigi, Kuta Mandalika, Selong Belanak, Gili Islands, and the entire West Nusa Tenggara. We also accept remote projects from international clients.

What services are offered?

We provide complete architectural services: house design, villas, commercial buildings, interior design, renovation, and design-and-build services (from design to finished construction).

Tell us what you're planning

You may already have land and a clear brief, or you may only have a few reference images and a question about where to begin. Tell us what you know so far, and we can work out the next useful step together.
