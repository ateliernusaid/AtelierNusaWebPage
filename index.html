What We Do

Good drawings begin with ordinary questions

Where is the access road? Which rooms need morning light? What will be hard to repair? How far can the budget stretch? We ask these questions before the pretty decisions become expensive ones.

Read the plot first

We look at access, slope, sun, wind, neighbours, and the views you want to keep. Those details tell us more than a reference image can.

Turn ideas into drawings

A render is only the beginning. We develop the layout, details, materials, and cost information needed to make the idea useful on site.

Stay close to the work

During construction, small questions arrive every day. We help check the decisions that affect orders, trades, quality, and the finished spaces.

Our starting point

A plot in Kuta is not a plot in Mataram

Coastal air, heavy rain, a steep approach, a busy street, and the people who will use the building all change the answer. We look at those conditions early, then decide what deserves attention first. Sometimes that means shade and airflow. Sometimes it means access, drainage, storage, or a more honest conversation about the budget.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they start

How much do architectural services cost in Lombok?

Our listed design reference is Rp 150,000 to Rp 350,000 per square metre. The final fee depends on the scope, complexity, and information the project needs.

How long does a project take?

There is no single answer. The sequence depends on the site, scope, approvals, materials, and how quickly decisions can be made. We discuss the likely sequence after reading the brief.

Do you work outside Mataram?

Yes. We work across Lombok, including Senggigi, Kuta Mandalika, Selong Belanak, the Gili Islands, and wider West Nusa Tenggara.

What can you help with?

We help with homes, villas, hospitality spaces, interiors, renovations, architectural design, and construction projects.
